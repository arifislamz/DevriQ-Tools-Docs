## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What is percent-encoding?**

A: Percent-encoding replaces unsafe or reserved ASCII characters with a '%' followed by their two-digit hexadecimal value.

**Q: Why are spaces encoded as `%20` or `+`?**

A: `%20` is the standard RFC 3986 encoding; `+` is commonly used in application/x-www-form-urlencoded query strings.

**Q: Is my URL or private token sent to a server?**

A: No, everything executes purely inside your local browser sandbox.
