## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What is the difference between UPC and EAN?**

A: UPC-A is a 12-digit standard primarily used in North America; EAN-13 is a 13-digit standard used across Europe and internationally.

**Q: Can I print these on sticky label sheets?**

A: Yes, the exported PNG and SVG files fit standard Avery or thermal label printers.

**Q: Is there any watermark on the downloaded barcodes?**

A: No, all barcodes are completely watermark-free and free for commercial use.
