## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Will blocking GPTBot stop my site from ranking on Google?**

A: No! GPTBot is OpenAI's training scraper; standard Googlebot is completely separate and continues indexing your site.

**Q: Where do I upload the generated file?**

A: Upload `robots.txt` directly to your domain's public root directory (e.g., `https://yourdomain.com/robots.txt`).

**Q: Do all bots obey robots.txt?**

A: Reputable crawlers (Google, Bing, OpenAI, Anthropic) strictly obey it; malicious scrapers may ignore it.
