## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Where do I place the generated sitemap?**

A: Upload `sitemap.xml` to the root directory of your website (e.g., `https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml`).

**Q: Does having an XML sitemap guarantee Google indexation?**

A: It guarantees search crawlers discover your URLs, but quality and relevance determine whether pages are indexed.

**Q: Is there a limit on how many URLs can be in a sitemap?**

A: A single standard sitemap file can hold up to 50,000 URLs and 50MB.
