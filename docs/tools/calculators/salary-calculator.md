## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How do you convert hourly wage to annual salary?**

A: Multiply the hourly rate by weekly hours (typically 40), then multiply by 52 weeks.

**Q: What is the difference between bi-weekly and semi-monthly?**

A: Bi-weekly is every two weeks (26 pay periods/year); semi-monthly is twice a month (24 pay periods/year).

**Q: Is my compensation data confidential?**

A: Entirely. Nothing is sent to servers; calculations run locally in your browser.
