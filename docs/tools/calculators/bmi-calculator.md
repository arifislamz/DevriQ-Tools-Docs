## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What is considered a healthy BMI?**

A: According to the World Health Organization (WHO), a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered normal/healthy weight.

**Q: Does BMI account for muscle mass?**

A: BMI is based on weight and height. Athletes with high muscle mass may have a higher BMI while maintaining low body fat.

**Q: Is my health data stored?**

A: No. All measurements stay strictly private in your browser.
