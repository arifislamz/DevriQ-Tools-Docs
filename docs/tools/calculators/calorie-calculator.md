## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What is BMR versus TDEE?**

A: BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the calories burned at complete rest. TDEE includes BMR plus physical movement and exercise.

**Q: How big of a caloric deficit is safe?**

A: A moderate deficit of 300–500 kcal/day typically yields 0.5–1 lb of sustainable fat loss per week.

**Q: Is any personal health data recorded?**

A: None at all. Your inputs remain entirely in your local browser session.
