## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What formula is used to calculate EMI?**

A: The standard reducing balance formula: EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1], where P is Principal, R is monthly rate, and N is tenure in months.

**Q: Can I use this for floating interest rates?**

A: Yes, enter your current floating interest rate to model expected installments.

**Q: Is my financial information recorded?**

A: Never. No financial inputs leave your browser sandbox.
