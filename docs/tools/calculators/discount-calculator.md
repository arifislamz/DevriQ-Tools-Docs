## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How do stacked discounts work?**

A: A stacked discount applies sequentially: a $100 item with 20% off becomes $80; an additional 10% off takes $8 off the $80, equaling $72.

**Q: Can I input fixed dollar discounts instead of percentages?**

A: Yes, the tool allows both percentage discounts and flat monetary reductions.

**Q: Is there any limit to calculations?**

A: None. Calculate as many retail bargains as you need.
