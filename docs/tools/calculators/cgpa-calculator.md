## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How is CGPA calculated from SGPA?**

A: CGPA is the weighted average of all semester SGPAs: Total (SGPA × Semester Credits) divided by Total Credits.

**Q: Can I convert 10-point CGPA to percentage?**

A: Yes, the standard UGC formula (CGPA × 9.5) and AICTE conversions are built directly into the tool.

**Q: Are my student records private?**

A: 100% private. DevriQ never stores, tracks, or shares your academic scores.
