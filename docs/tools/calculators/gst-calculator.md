## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How do I calculate GST exclusive amount?**

A: GST Exclusive: Base Amount + (Base Amount × GST%) = Total Price.

**Q: How do I extract GST from an inclusive price?**

A: GST Amount = Total Price - [Total Price × (100 / (100 + GST%))].

**Q: Does this work for international VAT?**

A: Yes, the calculation logic is mathematically identical for VAT and sales taxes.
